## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: Resharing two-phase confirmation protocol — on-chain coordination

FROST resharing SHALL use a two-phase protocol with all coordination state held on-chain. The coordinator (agent machine) drives Phase 1 and monitors Phase 2 events; the coordinator does NOT aggregate Phase 2 confirmations in memory.

**Phase 1 (Distribute and Acknowledge):**

New shares are generated by the current operator set and distributed to all N operators per the resharing DKG protocol. Old shares remain valid during this phase. Each operator, upon receiving its Phase 1 share, SHALL:

1. Validate the share against the resharing ceremony's Feldman VSS commitments (the same commitments broadcast in Round 1 of the resharing DKG). An operator that receives an invalid share SHALL broadcast a ceremony complaint; it SHALL NOT call `ackShareReceived`.
2. If the share is valid, call `ackShareReceived(epoch, sig)` on the AVS contract, where `sig` is an Ed25519 signature over `{ operator_id, epoch, action: "share_received_and_validated", vss_check: "pass" }` under the operator's registered AVS key.

The contract stores each receipt indexed by `(epoch, operator_id)`. These receipts are the evidence anchors for `slashNonConfirmation`.

**Phase 2 (Confirm):**

Within 30 minutes of Phase 1 completion, each operator SHALL call `confirmResharing(epoch, sig)` on the AVS contract, where `sig` covers `{ operator_id, epoch, action: "resharing_confirmed" }` under the operator's AVS key. The contract counts confirmations. When all N confirmations are received, the contract SHALL emit:

```
ResharingCompleted(epoch, block.timestamp, operator_set_hash)
```

where `operator_set_hash = keccak256(sorted operator_id array)` for the completing epoch. This event is the authoritative anchor for the 24-hour share deletion attestation window.

**Abort path:**

If fewer than N `confirmResharing` calls are received within 30 minutes of Phase 1 completion, any party MAY call `abortResharing(epoch)`. The contract verifies that the 30-minute window has elapsed and that not all N confirmations exist, then emits:

```
ResharingAborted(epoch)
```

Upon `ResharingAborted`, all operators retain their old shares. All new shares are discarded. The epoch counter does not advance. A new resharing ceremony must be initiated.

**Coordinator responsibility:** The coordinator initiates Phase 1 (sends encrypted shares to operators), then watches for `ResharingCompleted` or `ResharingAborted` events. Upon `ResharingCompleted`, the coordinator signals operators to delete old shares. No in-memory confirmation aggregation is required from the coordinator.

#### Scenario: Phase 1 ackShareReceived attests VSS validity, not mere delivery
- **WHEN** an operator receives its Phase 1 share but VSS validation fails
- **THEN** the operator broadcasts a ceremony complaint and does NOT call `ackShareReceived`; the missing receipt is not slashable (complaint is the correct response to an invalid share)

#### Scenario: Phase 2 confirmations are counted on-chain
- **WHEN** operators call `confirmResharing` within the 30-minute window
- **THEN** the contract counts each confirmation; when the N-th confirmation arrives, `ResharingCompleted` is emitted atomically in the same transaction

#### Scenario: ResharingCompleted anchors deletion attestation window
- **WHEN** `ResharingCompleted(epoch, timestamp, operator_set_hash)` is emitted
- **THEN** the 24-hour deletion attestation window begins at `timestamp`; operator deletion attestations submitted after `timestamp + 24 hours` are late and slashable

#### Scenario: Coordinator restart during Phase 2 does not lose state
- **WHEN** the coordinator process restarts at any point during Phase 2
- **THEN** all Phase 2 confirmation state is in the AVS contract; the coordinator re-reads on-chain state on restart; no progress is lost

#### Scenario: abortResharing before window elapses reverts
- **WHEN** `abortResharing(epoch)` is called before 30 minutes have elapsed since Phase 1 completion
- **THEN** the contract reverts; the ceremony continues

### Requirement: Phase 2 non-confirmation slashing (updated evidence format)

The AVS contract SHALL expose `slashNonConfirmation(operator_id, epoch)`. The contract SHALL:

1. Verify that an `ackShareReceived` record exists on-chain for `(epoch, operator_id)` — proving the operator received and validated its Phase 1 share.
2. Verify that no `confirmResharing` record exists on-chain for `(epoch, operator_id)` — proving the operator did not confirm.
3. Verify that 30 minutes have elapsed since Phase 1 completion for `epoch`.
4. Slash the operator's EigenLayer stake.

No off-chain `signed_share_receipt` parameter is needed — the on-chain `ackShareReceived` record is the evidence. The contract reads the stored receipt directly.

#### Scenario: slashNonConfirmation uses on-chain ackShareReceived as evidence
- **WHEN** `slashNonConfirmation(operator_id, epoch)` is called
- **THEN** the contract reads the on-chain `ackShareReceived` record (not a caller-supplied signature), confirms no `confirmResharing` exists, confirms 30 minutes elapsed, and slashes

#### Scenario: Operator that confirmed on time cannot be slashed
- **WHEN** `slashNonConfirmation` is called for an operator that submitted `confirmResharing` within the window
- **THEN** the on-chain `confirmResharing` record exists; the contract reverts with a confirmation-exists error
